TGGB Help

Goal

Hop across the isometric pyramid. Every safe landing lights that cube. Light the whole pyramid to advance to the next level.

Movement

G-Bert moves only diagonally across adjacent cubes. Use the four diagonal buttons, Q/E/Z/C, or the arrow keys. Each move is a committed hop, so timing matters.

Globs

Globs are original bouncing hazards that enter from the top and hop down the pyramid. Touching one costs a life. They become more frequent as the levels increase.

Falling

A hop outside the pyramid sends G-Bert tumbling into space and costs a life. After a short pause, the character returns to the top if any lives remain.

Face Image

Load one local image, crop the face, and TGGB draws it onto the player character. The image is decoded locally and is not uploaded or stored.

Privacy

The loaded image exists only for this page session. Reloading or closing the page removes it.
